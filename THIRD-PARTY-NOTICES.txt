ORIGINAL ROSTER CUSTOMIZATION
All seven wrestlers derive from the Loves_Art generic male/female meshes above.
Original changes authored for this project: front/back gear UVs; 2048px color and
roughness maps; editable SVG patterns and insignias; modeled brush cut, spikes,
bun, mohawk, cornrows, braid and fitted mask; wraps, knee/elbow pads, ear hoops
and wrist studs; per-character palettes, skin tones and proportions. Accessories
are weighted to the generic rigs. Source body attribution remains required.
The earlier Idmenthal heavyweight and its derived assets have been removed.

The production wrestling ring, folding chairs, PA cabinet, lighting truss, fog
machine, venue surface shaders, interface, and game code were authored for this
project. Blender source and reproducible build script are included. The earlier
TepidGames ring remains in the source archive; the production ring is now used.
